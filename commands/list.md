---
description: "List open and past temporary whiteboard sites and their invite links"
argument-hint: "[<id>]"
---
Arguments: $ARGUMENTS. Without an id, run `tempboard list`; with an id, run `tempboard links <id>`. Show the output as is (if `tempboard` is not on PATH, use `python3 "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/tempboard"`).
